- 与公司直属上司沟通薪资情况,并努力争取薪资的提升(Talk with your direct supervisor about your salary situation and try to improve your salary)

**挑战:**
1. 和上司沟通现有对公司的贡献 
    Talk with your direct supervisor about your contributions to the company.
2. 沟通现有薪资没有达到预期,并努力争取更高的薪资  
    Communicate your current salary situation and try to improve it.
3. 讨论你的职业目标，并已经达到了下个阶段的要求  
    Discuss your career goals and how you have met them in the next phase.
